- Sharper icons along the bottom: the ones you're not on are no longer fuzzy.
- Categories: press and hold a category in the category list to favourite, rename or hide it.
- Picture: a new Picture button in the player lets you choose Fit, Fill screen, Stretch, 16:9 or 4:3. It's remembered for each channel, film and series.
- Pinch to zoom: pinch out on the video to fill the screen, pinch in to see the whole picture.
- "Because you watched" now only uses films and series you actually watched (finished, or at least 5 minutes in), not ones you only opened or started by accident.
- Skip Recap: alongside Skip Intro, from TheIntroDB. The player's Audio & Subtitles menu shows what it found for each episode.
- The player: Start Over, Episodes and Next Episode under the picture, and a settings button at the top.
- Film and series pages, Netflix-style: Resume with its progress and Play From Beginning, or Play and Audio & Subtitles for something new. Something you're part way through plays in the picture at the top.
- Because you watched: press and hold a card for "Don't recommend based on this".
